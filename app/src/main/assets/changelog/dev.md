### Sound Sync (Beta)
Turn your phone into a live audio-reactive controller for your WLED devices. Capture sound from your microphone, and the app sends real-time frequency data and beat detection to all your WLED devices over UDP Sound Sync. Your LED strips will dance to the beat, no extra hardware required. Enable Sound Sync (Beta) in Settings to try it early, and make sure your devices are set to Receive mode in their audio sync settings.
