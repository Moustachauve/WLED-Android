### 🪄 Shiny New Widgets
Control lights without even opening the app! These redesigned widgets are interactive, sync in real-time, and look stunning with dynamic colors.

### ⚡ Quick Control
Flick the switch directly from Android Quick Settings. It's like having a dedicated WLED remote built right into the phone's notification shade.

### 🚀 To the Moon and Beyond!
Added OTA update support for **QuinLED** and **MoonModules** boards. Keeping hardware up-to-date has never been this satisfying, and even more custom repositories are coming in future releases to expand these capabilities further!

### 🔗 Deep Magic
Support for `wled://` links is here! Hop directly to a favorite device from other apps or shortcuts. 

### 🌍 Global Glow
WLED-Android now speaks German and Chinese! The auto-discovery engine is also more polished, and a bunch of bugs have been squashed to make everything smoother than ever.
