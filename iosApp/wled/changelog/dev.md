### Latest Dev Changes

This update brings you:
- **Theme Selection**: Choose between System Default, Light, and Dark appearance directly in Settings.
- **Offline Device Grouping**: Toggle "Show Offline Devices Last" in Settings to either group offline devices in their own section at the bottom or mix them alphabetically with online devices.
- **Custom Firmware Updates**: Added OTA update support for **QuinLED** and **MoonModules** boards alongside official WLED releases.
- **Smoother Connections & Discovery**: Unified real-time WebSocket management, faster mDNS device discovery, and a 60-second offline grace period so devices don't flicker into the offline list during brief reconnects.

Enjoy your lights!
