### What's New Changelog
Automatically see release notes after an app update or revisit them anytime from Settings.

### Theme Selection
Choose between System Default, Light, and Dark appearance directly in Settings.

### Offline Device Grouping
Toggle "Show Offline Devices Last" in Settings to either group offline devices in their own section at the bottom or mix them alphabetically with online devices.

### Automatic Discovery Setting
Prefer a quieter network? Turn off "Automatically Discover New Devices" in Settings and the app will only look for new lights when you pull down on the device list. Leave it on and the app now also scans whenever you come back to it.

### Custom Firmware Updates
Added OTA update support for **QuinLED** and **MoonModules** boards alongside official WLED releases.

### Smoother Connections & Discovery
Unified real-time WebSocket management, faster mDNS device discovery, and a 60-second offline grace period so devices don't flicker into the offline list during brief reconnects.

### Sound Sync
Turn your WLED devices into a music visualizer. Sound Sync captures audio from your microphone and sends real-time frequency data to your devices over the local network using UDP Sound Sync. Adjust the gain, enable automatic gain control, and watch the live frequency bars react to the sound around you.
